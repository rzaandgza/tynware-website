Handyman quotes with a clear view of your margin.

Price materials and labour, reuse common quote items and export a professional customer PDF. Offer Desk is Windows quoting software for independent tradespeople.

Explore Offer DeskSee the worked example

Turn a repair job into a quote you can check.

A small repair job can combine materials, several hours of labour and extra services. A total copied from an old document can hide a changed material cost or a different amount of work. Offer Desk keeps internal costs separate from the price you show the customer, so you can review the numbers before sending the quote.

It fits an independent handyman or small contractor who prepares quotes on a Windows PC and wants customers, reusable items and job pricing in one focused tool.

Worked example · Fictional data

Materials plus four hours of finishing work.

In the sample, materials cost EUR 240 internally and are sold for EUR 320. Labour costs EUR 40 per hour internally; four hours are sold at EUR 53.33 per hour. The internal cost is EUR 400 and the customer price is EUR 533.32. The difference is EUR 133.32, a margin of approximately 25% before other costs or tax.

Line prices are rounded to cents, so this is slightly different from the EUR 533.33 produced by a total-cost calculation targeting exactly 25%. Check the resulting margin after rounding your customer prices.

Reuse items while checking each new job.

Keep frequently used materials, labour and services in the Library. Reuse customer details and quote lines, then review quantities and selling prices for the current scope. Repetition should save typing without hiding the details that changed.

Offer Desk supports eight interface and quote/PDF languages, selected independently. Structural labels are translated; your own descriptions and terms remain as entered. Review that text before sending a document in another language.

A focused quoting workflow on Windows.

Core customer and pricing data stays on your PC, with backup/restore and CSV export. The supported platform is Windows 11 x64. Offer Desk is focused on job pricing and quotes, rather than accounting, payment collection, site measurements or a mobile field-service workflow.
