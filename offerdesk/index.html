Offer Desk · Windows

Price the job. Know your profit. Send the quote.

Offer Desk is a focused job-pricing and quoting app for independent tradespeople and small contractors. Build a quote from real costs, see profit and margin before you send it, and produce a professional customer PDF without adopting a full ERP or CRM.

A quote starts with the real job cost

Know the number before you send the number.

Offer Desk keeps internal costing separate from the customer-facing quote, so you can work from material and service costs, set the selling price, and immediately see the resulting profit and margin.

  • Internal cost, customer price, profit and margin.
  • Target margin with a recommended minimum selling price.
  • Reusable materials and services library with price snapshots.
  • Customer records and quote history with duplication.
  • Company profile and professional PDF output.
  • Backup, export and local recovery workflow.

Built to stay focused

Quoting software, not a business-management platform.

Offer Desk deliberately avoids inventory management, scheduling, accounting, CRM pipelines and mandatory cloud collaboration. The goal is a faster, clearer path from job cost to customer quote.
