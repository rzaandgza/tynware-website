FAQ

Questions before you try it.

Is Offer Desk cloud software?

No. Offer Desk is a Windows desktop application and its core customer, quote and pricing data is stored locally on your PC.

Is Offer Desk a CRM or accounting system?

No. It is intentionally focused on job pricing and quoting. It does not try to replace a full CRM, accounting package, scheduling system or inventory platform.

Does the customer see my internal cost or margin?

No. Internal cost, estimated profit and margin stay inside Offer Desk. The customer-facing PDF contains the selling price.

Does Offer Desk translate everything I type?

No. Structural interface and document labels are localized. Your own descriptions, notes and terms remain exactly as you enter them.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Romanian, Hungarian and Polish are supported for both the interface and quote/PDF structure.

Can I back up or export my data?

Yes. Offer Desk includes local backup/restore and CSV export tools.
