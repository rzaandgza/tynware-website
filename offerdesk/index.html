FAQ

Questions before you try it.

How does the free trial and purchase work?

Download and install the same signed Windows 11 x64 application without a Tynware account, payment card, purchase or license key. You receive 14 days of full access from first use. After expiry, the application remains accessible for viewing, backup and data export; editing, imports and restore require activation. Your local records are preserved. Buy License opens the Offer Desk Lemon Squeezy Live checkout directly. Lemon Squeezy delivers your key after purchase. Choose Activate License in the sidebar or Settings → Manage license in the same installed application. No reinstall or data migration is needed.

Is VAT or sales tax included in the price?

No. The listed €113 is the price before applicable taxes. Depending on your country, Lemon Squeezy may add VAT or sales tax at checkout. The final total, including any applicable taxes, is shown before you pay.

Which Windows versions are officially supported?

The public release targets Windows 11 on x64 (64-bit) Intel/AMD PCs. Windows 10 is not currently in the official support matrix because this release has not been validated there, and macOS is not currently available.

Is Offer Desk cloud software?

No. Offer Desk is a Windows desktop application and its core customer, quote and pricing data is stored locally on your PC.

Is Offer Desk a CRM or accounting system?

No. It is intentionally focused on job pricing and quoting. It does not try to replace a full CRM, accounting package, scheduling system or inventory platform.

Does the customer see my internal cost or margin?

No. Internal cost, estimated profit and margin stay inside Offer Desk. The customer-facing PDF contains the selling price.

Does Offer Desk translate everything I type?

No. Structural interface and document labels are localized. Your own descriptions, notes and terms remain exactly as you enter them.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Romanian, Hungarian and Polish are supported for both the interface and quote/PDF structure.

Can I back up or export my data?

Yes. Offer Desk includes local backup/restore and CSV export tools.
