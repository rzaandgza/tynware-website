Refund policy

Refunds, withdrawal and digital software.

This policy explains how refund requests are handled for Tynware software. Mandatory consumer rights always take priority over this policy.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Commercial checkout is not yet enabled. This policy is prepared for launch and will take effect together with the completed company details and live checkout.

1. No blanket money-back promise

Unless a specific offer expressly states otherwise, Tynware does not advertise a general no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. This does not affect statutory withdrawal, conformity, refund, repair, replacement or termination rights that apply under mandatory law.

2. Lemon Squeezy purchases

Paid Tynware purchases are planned to be processed by Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record. Refunds for those transactions are issued through Lemon Squeezy. Requests are assessed under applicable law, Lemon Squeezy's Buyer Terms and this Tynware policy. Lemon Squeezy may also issue a refund under its own terms and risk/chargeback procedures.

Lemon Squeezy Buyer Terms

3. EU/EEA Consumer withdrawal rights

If you are a Consumer purchasing at a distance, you may have a statutory withdrawal period. For digital content supplied without a tangible medium, the withdrawal right may be lost once supply begins only where the applicable legal requirements are met, including prior express consent to immediate performance, acknowledgement of the resulting loss of the withdrawal right, and the required contractual confirmation.

If those requirements are not met, this policy does not remove or shorten the statutory withdrawal right.

4. Defective or non-conforming software

If the software is defective or does not conform to mandatory legal requirements, contact support with enough information to reproduce the problem. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, the appropriate remedy may include bringing the software into conformity, replacement, price reduction, termination or refund.

5. Courtesy refunds

Tynware may support a courtesy refund in appropriate cases, for example where a genuine technical incompatibility was not reasonably apparent before purchase and support cannot provide a practical remedy. Courtesy refunds are discretionary and case-specific; granting one does not create an entitlement to future refunds.

6. Business purchases

Business Users do not receive Consumer withdrawal rights unless mandatory law gives them equivalent protection. Business refund requests may still be considered case by case, including where the software cannot be delivered or a verified technical issue cannot reasonably be remedied.

7. How to request a refund

Email support@tynware.com with the product name, order email, order/reference number if available, purchase date and a short explanation. Do not send payment-card details or your full license key.

Where the order was processed by Lemon Squeezy, Tynware may direct the request through the Merchant of Record so the transaction can be refunded correctly.
