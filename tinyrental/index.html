FAQ

Questions before you try it.

Is TinyRental cloud software?

No. TinyRental is a Windows desktop application and its core business data is stored locally on your PC.

Does TinyRental need an internet connection?

The core inventory and rental workflow is local-first. Internet access is used for license activation and periodic validation.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Your data remains available. TinyRental switches to read-only mode until a license is activated; backup and export remain available.

How many computers can I activate?

A TinyRental lifetime license currently allows up to two active devices.

Is this accounting software?

No. Financial reports show booked reservation value. TinyRental does not currently track payments, taxes, invoices or accounting ledgers.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. TinyRental includes verified backup/restore and user-readable CSV export.
