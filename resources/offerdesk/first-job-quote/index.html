Your first job quote in Offer Desk.

Prepare a materials-and-labour quote, check internal cost and margin, then review the customer PDF before sending it.

Explore Offer DeskSee the worked example

Prepare a small practice quote.

When the release is available, get the installer from the official Offer Desk product page. Follow its supported-platform and installation guidance. Use fictional customer details for this example, and create a backup first if you already have working records.

The example describes a small interior repair. The names, rates and terms are illustrative; choose your own prices and terms for real work.

1. Review your company and document settings.

Check the issuer details, currency, document language, tax treatment and default terms. The customer should be able to identify who issued the quote and what work the price covers.

2. Add the customer and scope.

Create a fictional customer and a quote titled Sample interior repair job. Describe what is included in the work, rather than relying on an ambiguous line such as General work. Confirm the quote date and validity period.

3. Enter materials and labour.

4. Review the internal result.

The cost is EUR 400, selling price EUR 533.32 and difference EUR 133.32, approximately 25% margin on the costs entered. Any omitted overhead or other expense still affects what you earn. Rounding individual line prices can change the final margin.

Use the markup versus margin guide if you are choosing a selling price from a target margin.

5. Check the customer PDF.

Export a quote and open it before sending. Confirm descriptions, quantities, selling prices, validity and terms. The PDF should contain the customer prices without the internal cost or margin calculation. This example uses EUR and applies no sales tax.

6. Reuse and back up.

Keep often-used materials, labour and services in the Library. For future quotes, review the quantities and selling prices against the new scope rather than assuming the previous job is identical. Create a local backup after useful setup work.

Working across languages.

Interface and document language are independent. Document labels can be localised into eight supported languages, but your descriptions, notes and terms are not automatically translated. Review the entire customer document in its chosen language.
