2. What this notice covers

This notice covers tynware.com, product support communications, license activation and validation, and any personal data Tynware receives in connection with TinyRental, Offer Desk or future Tynware products. A product may add a specific privacy note when its data flows differ materially from this baseline.

3. Local-first product data

If you voluntarily send a backup, screenshot, log or other file to support, the information contained in that file becomes part of the support request. Please redact customer, payment, health, confidential or other unnecessary personal data before sending it.

4. Personal data we may process

Website technical data

IP address, request time, requested URL, browser/device information, security signals and similar connection metadata may be processed by our hosting and security providers to deliver and protect the site.

Support data

Your email address, name if supplied, product/version information, message content, diagnostic details and any attachments you choose to send.

Licensing data

For products that use online licensing, this can include the license key, activation instance identifiers, activation status, validation timestamps and product/license metadata. TinyRental uses a randomly generated activation instance rather than uploading its operational business database.

Purchase data

When checkout is enabled, the Merchant of Record processes your payment. Tynware may receive order, customer, product, license, refund and transaction-status information needed to deliver and support the software. Tynware does not receive or store your full payment-card number.

6. Website analytics and cookies

At the date of this notice, Tynware does not intentionally deploy advertising pixels or behavioral advertising trackers on tynware.com. We do not sell personal data. Infrastructure providers may process technical request data and may use strictly necessary technologies for security, routing, abuse prevention or service operation. If Tynware later introduces non-essential analytics or marketing technologies, this notice and any consent mechanism will be updated before they are enabled where consent is required.

7. Service providers and recipients

We use a limited number of providers where needed to operate Tynware:

  • Cloudflare for website hosting, delivery, DNS and security. Cloudflare may process connection and traffic metadata needed to provide those services.
  • Lemon Squeezy, when checkout/licensing is enabled, as Merchant of Record and/or licensing provider. Lemon Squeezy handles payment processing, applicable transaction taxes, refunds/chargebacks and associated checkout data under its own terms and privacy notice.
  • Email hosting/routing providers for receiving and sending support correspondence.
  • Professional advisers, authorities or courts where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not provide product operational databases to these providers as part of the normal local-first workflow.

8. International transfers

Some service providers may process data outside Romania or the European Economic Area. Where GDPR requires safeguards for an international transfer, we rely on the provider's applicable transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards.

9. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected:

  • support correspondence is normally retained for up to 24 months after the request is resolved, unless a longer period is needed for an ongoing issue or legal claim;
  • license and entitlement records are retained while needed to provide and verify the license and for a reasonable period afterward for fraud prevention, support and legal claims;
  • transaction or legal records are retained for the period required by applicable law or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
  • website/security logs follow the retention settings of the relevant infrastructure service and are kept only as needed for delivery, diagnostics and security.

Local product data stored only on your device is controlled by you and is not subject to Tynware's server-side retention schedule.

10. Your GDPR rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send requests to support@tynware.com. We may need enough information to verify the request and protect other people from unauthorized disclosure. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP, or another competent supervisory authority in the EU/EEA.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect data handled by Tynware. Local-first storage reduces the amount of operational business data transmitted to us, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. You remain responsible for securing your Windows account, device, backups and exported files.

12. Automated decisions and children

Tynware does not currently use personal data collected through the website, support or licensing to make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means. Tynware business software is not directed to children.

13. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when products, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes that affect an active service will be communicated in an appropriate way.
