Privacy at Tynware

TinyRental is designed around local-first storage. Operational rental data is stored on the user's Windows PC and is not uploaded to Tynware as part of normal use.

TinyRental application

License activation and periodic validation communicate with the licensing provider to verify license status. TinyRental's backup and CSV export features operate on local files selected by the user.

Website and checkout

The website host may process standard technical request data needed to serve the site. Purchases are handled by the checkout / merchant-of-record provider, which processes payment and transaction information under its own privacy terms.
